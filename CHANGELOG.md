# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

### Rentals

- Products with serial-tracked units, QR labels and scanning, categories, brands, kits and locations.
- Availability search and calendar; bookings that cannot double-book a unit.
- Quotes, reservations, check-out and check-in with inspections, photos and signatures.
- Rental contracts and invoices as PDF, invoices by email, invoices marked as paid.
- Deposits and their settlement, late fees, damage records.
- Maintenance plans and tasks, stock transfers between locations.
- Customers with documents.

### Administration

- Dashboard with today's pick-ups and returns, overdue rentals, fleet status, unpaid invoices and a six-month revenue chart.
- Reports: utilization, revenue, damage and overdue, with CSV export.
- Settings screen for company name, currency, tax, invoice due days, late fees, default language, date and time format and public booking.
- Users screen for administrators, with the roles admin, manager and staff.
- Languages managed by the administrator, with English and Bengali included and JSON import and export for new ones. Right-to-left languages are supported.
- Public booking-request form with spam protection and rate limits.
- Web installer, with optional sample data.
